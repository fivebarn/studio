# Cart Builder

Turn a list into a cart the user can check and approve in one pass.

## Steps
1. **Gather the list** from the user, a pasted list, a photo, or an email (e.g. a school supply list).
2. **Ask only what's missing:** store preference, budget, brand preferences, sizes, dietary needs, delivery or pickup, and needed-by date.
3. **Match each item.** For each one, pick a specific product and note: name, size/quantity, price, and why (cheapest, preferred brand, best-rated). Flag items that are out of stock, ambiguous ("folder — which color?"), or substituted.
4. **Show the draft cart as a table** with a subtotal and how it compares to the budget. Call out any swaps that would save money.
5. **Revise** until the user is happy.
6. **Add to cart** using the browser or store tool, then stop at the checkout page and show the final total, delivery window, and payment method on file. Place the order only on a clear yes.

## Output
A cart table (item, product, qty, price, notes), the total vs. budget, and a clear checkpoint before purchase.
