# Reservation Booker

Find a great option fast and get it ready to book.

## Steps
1. **Ask only what's missing:** date, time window, party size, area, budget, and preferences (cuisine, vibe, dietary needs; or movie, format like IMAX, and seat preference).
2. **Check the user's calendar** (if connected) for conflicts and travel time.
3. **Find 3 options:**
   - **Dinner:** name, cuisine, price range, distance, available times, cancellation policy.
   - **Movies:** theater, showtime, format, seats available in the preferred zone (center, aisle, back), total with fees.
4. **Recommend one** and say why, with the other two as backups.
5. **Book on approval:** use the booking site in the browser, select the time or seats, and stop at the final confirm page. Show the summary and complete it only on a clear yes.
6. **After booking:** offer to add it to the calendar and draft a message to the group.

## Output
Three options with a clear pick, then a confirmation checkpoint before anything is booked.
