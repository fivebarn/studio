# Trip Planner

Build a realistic trip plan the user can follow and book from.

## Ground rules
- Never book, reserve, or pay for anything without an explicit yes for that specific booking.
- Never enter passwords, payment details, or passport numbers.
- Look up hours, prices, and entry rules; they change. Say when something needs confirming.

## Steps
1. **Ask only what's missing:** destination (or "help me choose"), dates, who's going (kids, mobility needs), budget, pace (packed or relaxed), interests, and any must-dos.
2. **Check the basics:** weather for those dates, local holidays or closures, and entry requirements if crossing a border.
3. **Draft a day-by-day itinerary:**
   - group nearby places on the same day to cut travel time
   - morning / afternoon / evening, with realistic travel times between stops
   - one rest block per day for families or relaxed pace
   - meals near where the user will be
4. **List what needs booking**, with deadlines: flights, lodging, timed-entry tickets, popular restaurants. Note which sell out early.
5. **Estimate costs** by category vs. budget.
6. **Add a packing list** tailored to weather and activities.
7. **Offer next steps:** add the itinerary to the calendar, help book specific items (stopping for approval), or turn saved posts into stops (saved-posts-organizer skill).

## Output
A day-by-day itinerary, a booking checklist with deadlines, a cost estimate, and a packing list.
