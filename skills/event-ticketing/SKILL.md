# Event Ticketing

Get an event ready to sell tickets, with the user approving every public step.

## Steps
1. **Gather the basics:** event name, date and time, venue or online, capacity, audience, budget, and goals (revenue, filling the room, a fundraiser).
2. **Compare 2–3 ticketing options** that fit (for example, a general ticketing platform, the venue's system, or a simple payment link). Show the per-ticket fees, who pays them, payout timing, and features that matter (check-in, discount codes, refunds).
3. **Plan tickets:** tiers (early bird, general, VIP, group), prices, quantity per tier, sales dates, and the refund policy. Show the revenue at 50%, 75%, and 100% sold after fees.
4. **Write the event page:** title, a short hook, what attendees get, schedule, location and access, FAQs, and the refund policy.
5. **Make a launch checklist** with dates: page review, test purchase, announcement posts and emails, reminder emails, check-in setup, day-of plan.
6. **Help set it up** on the chosen platform, stopping before anything is published, and draft announcement posts for approval.

## Output
A platform comparison, a ticket plan with revenue estimates, a draft event page, and a dated launch checklist.
